# macworx

Extra homework practice that kids actually want to come back to.

macworx turns practice into a game. Kids work through short assignments on a tablet and earn **Macaronis**, a currency they spend in a **rewards shop** on real-world rewards their parents set up, like ice cream or a trip. Doing a little every day builds a **streak**, and a longer streak earns bonus Macaronis.

It's built around one rule: effort earns rewards, and nothing is ever taken away. A missed answer just shows how the problem works, then she moves on.

## What's in it

- **Assignments** in school subjects, starting with math; reading, spelling and phonics come next.
- **Macaronis** for every right answer, plus bonuses for accuracy and streaks.
- **Daily streaks** for practicing day after day.
- **A rewards shop** where Macaronis turn into real rewards.
- **A parent dashboard** to see progress, pair devices and adjust anything.
- **Works offline** on an iPad, installed from Safari to the home screen.
